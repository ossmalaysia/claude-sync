# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and do not harass
anyone. Harassment includes offensive comments, personal attacks, publishing
others' private information, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/ossmalaysia/claude-sync/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [Anchor Sprint](https://www.anchorsprint.com/). All reports
are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions, and may ban contributors, who do not follow this code.
